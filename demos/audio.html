all sounds

Every one-shot plays with the gain and pitch the game would use at a soft, medium or hard level. Impacts use the game's current mapping or the proposed one. Positional sounds play 3 m ahead and to the left. Loops sit at the origin; the distance slider moves the listener away from them (the hum should be faint by 10 m and gone by 16 m).

room and occlusion

The room sets the reverb character (cross-faded over 0.4 s); the game picks it from the level's acoustic zones. Occlusion applies to every loop playing now (gliding) and to every one-shot played from now on: 0 is a clear line of sight, 1 is behind walls (dull, -14 dB, relatively more reverb).

contact loops: drag

Each row is a body sliding or rolling 2.5 m ahead. "drag" starts it; speed (m/s) and contact force (N) drive contactLoopParams live, exactly as the game will. Below 0.08 m/s it is silent. The push buttons feed a simulated shove through the game's voice pool (createContactVoicePool): speed jumps up, then friction brings the body to rest.
